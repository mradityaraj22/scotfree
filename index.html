    Good to know

    Hostel prices, student discounts and bus rules.

    Free bus rules

    What the card covers

    • Free: Lothian, East Coast, Borders Buses, Citylink, Stagecoach, McGill's, First, and local buses on islands.
    • Not free: trams, trains, ferries, tour buses, airport express, anything into England.
    • November: dark by 4pm, so leave by 7–9am and check the last bus back.
    • Citylink: seats can't be booked on a free pass. Arrive 15 minutes early.

    Hostel prices

    November, per night

    Student discounts

    £ under £10 · ££ £10–20 · £££ £20+

    • Always carry your matric card and Young Scot card, and ask for the student price.
    • Young Scot app: Rewards and Discounts list local deals and free tickets.
    • Castles (HES): student tickets everywhere. Doing 3+ castles on one trip? Get the Explorer Pass.
    • National Trust for Scotland: cheap Young Person membership (under 26) pays off after 2–3 visits.
    • Edinburgh Zoo + Highland Wildlife Park: student tickets, and one RZSS membership covers both.
    • UNiDAYS, Student Beans, TOTUM: food and cinema deals on trips.

    Animal seasons

    When to see what

    • Oct–Dec: seal pups at St Abbs, geese at Aberlady, Loch Leven, Montrose, Caerlaverock and Islay. Deer near roads in Glencoe.
    • Apr–Jul: puffins (Isle of May, Lunga, Orkney), ospreys, gannets on Bass Rock.
    • All year: red squirrels, Eyemouth seals, Aberdeen dolphins, Highland cows.

    Insta spots with no bus

    Plan these differently

    • Fairy Pools, Neist Point (Skye): cheap minibus tour from Portree.
    • Glen Etive, Applecross, Sandwood Bay: no or very rare buses.
    • Steam train, Isle of May, Staffa: paid and summer only.

    Room or two dorm beds?

    Saving tips for two

    • Two dorm beds cost £40–60. A Premier Inn or Travelodge room is often £35–60 for both off-season, especially Sunday to Thursday.
    • Many rural hostels close November to March. Check before booking.
    • Choose hostels with a kitchen and cook dinner.
