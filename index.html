Where to sleep

Hostel prices.

Typical November prices. Dorm is per person; a private room is for both of you.

    Room or two dorm beds?

    • Two dorm beds cost £40–60. A Premier Inn or Travelodge room is often £35–60 for both off-season, especially Sunday to Thursday.
    • Many hostels have private twin rooms too.

    Before you book

    • Many rural hostels close November to March. Check first.
    • Book on syha.org.uk or Hostelworld.
    • Pick hostels with a kitchen and cook dinner.

    Save more

    Student discounts.

    Carry your matric card and Young Scot card, and always ask for the student price. Rough adult prices: £ under £10, ££ £10–20, £££ £20+.

    Young Scot app

    • The Rewards and Discounts pages list local deals and free tickets.
    • Look for the Young Scot sticker in cafés and shops.

    Castles (HES)

    • Student tickets at Edinburgh, Stirling, Urquhart, Tantallon, Linlithgow, Doune, Skara Brae.
    • Three or more castles on one trip? The Explorer Pass is usually cheaper.

    National Trust for Scotland

    • Cheap Young Person membership (under 26) pays off after 2–3 visits.
    • Covers Culzean, Culloden, Brodick Castle, Falkland Palace, Glencoe and Glenfinnan centres.

    Zoos and wildlife

    • Edinburgh Zoo + Highland Wildlife Park share one membership (RZSS). Both have student tickets.
    • Five Sisters Zoo, Deep Sea World, Deer Centre: ask for student prices and book online.
    • RSPB reserve trails are mostly free.

    Animal seasons

    • Oct–Dec: seal pups, geese, deer near roads.
    • Apr–Jul: puffins, ospreys, gannets.
    • All year: red squirrels, Eyemouth seals, Aberdeen dolphins, Highland cows.

    Insta spots with no bus

    • Fairy Pools, Neist Point (Skye): join a cheap minibus tour from Portree.
    • Glen Etive, Applecross, Sandwood Bay: no or very rare buses.
    • Steam train, Isle of May, Staffa: paid, summer only.

    ✅ Free with the card

    • Lothian, East Coast Buses, Borders Buses
    • Scottish Citylink coaches
    • Stagecoach, McGill's, First, West Coast Motors
    • Local buses on the islands

    ❌ Not free

    • Trams and trains
    • Ferries and tour buses
    • Anything crossing into England
    • Airport express buses

    🌙 November rules

    • Dark by 4pm. Leave by 7–9am.
    • Check the last bus back before you go.
    • Citylink: arrive 15 minutes early, seats can't be booked on a free pass.
    Bus numbers, times and prices are approximate and change. Check Traveline Scotland the day before, especially the last bus home.
